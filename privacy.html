Privacy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

What WindowWarp does with your data

WindowWarp sends the picture and sound of your computer to your TV or phone inside your own home network. That stream never goes to us or to anyone else. We have no servers, no accounts and no analytics.

What stays on your devices

  • The name of your computer and the list of paired devices.
  • Your settings, such as picture quality and language.
  • The start date of the free trial.

You can remove a paired device at any time with Forget, or by uninstalling the app.

Connections outside your home

  • To set up the connection, both apps ask a public server from Google (stun.l.google.com) for their network address. Only network addresses are exchanged, no picture or sound.
  • Purchases in the TV and phone app go through Google Play. Google's privacy policy applies to those payments. We only receive whether you have paid.
