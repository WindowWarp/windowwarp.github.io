WindowWarp for Linux

Free. One file, nothing else to install. Works on KDE Plasma and GNOME on Wayland, and on the Steam Deck in desktop mode.

Download AppImage

Install

  1. Download WindowWarp-x86_64.AppImage and put it where you want to keep it, for example in your home folder.
  2. Make it executable: right-click › Properties › Permissions › Allow executing file as program. Or in a terminal: chmod +x WindowWarp-x86_64.AppImage
  3. Double-click it. The first time, your desktop asks which screen to share: pick one and allow it. WindowWarp remembers your choice.
  4. Optional: under General, turn on Start when you sign in. WindowWarp then waits in the tray.

Then on your TV, phone or tablet

Install WindowWarp Receiver from Google Play, choose Search on the device, pick your computer and click Allow on the computer. From then on it connects by itself.

What you need

  • A Wayland desktop: KDE Plasma or GNOME. Steam Deck in desktop mode works too.
  • A graphics card from NVIDIA, AMD or Intel, for fast encoding.
  • A 64-bit PC (x86_64) with a recent distribution, such as Ubuntu 24.04, Linux Mint 22, Fedora or Arch.
  • The computer and the device on the same home network, by Wi-Fi or cable.

Updates

WindowWarp checks for a new version when it starts. A line at the top of the window tells you, and one click installs it and restarts the app.

Problems

Nothing happens when I double-click it
The file is not executable yet. See step 2.
The device does not find my computer
Check that both are on the same network, and that a firewall allows TCP port 5859 and UDP port 5860.
I moved the AppImage and it no longer starts when I sign in
Open it once from its new place. Start when you sign in follows it.

Remove

Turn off Start when you sign in, quit WindowWarp from the tray and delete the AppImage file. Your settings are in ~/.config/rdv88/windowwarp.conf and the folder ~/.config/windowwarp; delete those too if you want nothing left behind.
