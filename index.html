Questions

Does it work over the internet?

No. Your computer and device have to be on the same home network. That is what keeps the delay this low.

The TV finds my computer but shows no picture

A VPN on the TV often blocks the connection. Add WindowWarp to the exceptions (split tunneling) in your VPN app. WindowWarp tells you when this happens.

Wi-Fi or cable?

Both work. A cable is steadier, Wi-Fi 5 GHz is usually fine. You can see which one is used under Devices in the computer app.

Can I hear the sound only on the TV?

Yes. In the computer app you choose whether the sound plays on the computer, the TV or both.
